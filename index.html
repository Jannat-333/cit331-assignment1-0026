📚 Book Club – Where Stories Bring Us Together The Book Club is a welcoming space for students who love reading, exploring new stories, and sharing ideas. Whether you're a fan of fiction, romance, mystery, thrillers, or personal development, our club brings together people with different reading interests. Through book discussions, reading challenges, literary activities, and interactive sessions, we aim to make reading more enjoyable, encourage creativity, and build a community of book lovers. You don't have to be an avid reader to join—just bring your curiosity and a love for stories!
### 📚 Featured Events 1. Literary Debate Discuss literary themes, character development, and different interpretations of famous works. 2. Poetry & Prose Open Mic Share original writing, recite poetry, and celebrate the art of literature. 3. Book of the Month Explore a selected book through group discussions and critical analysis. 4. Book Swap Day Exchange books with fellow readers and discover new authors and genres.
